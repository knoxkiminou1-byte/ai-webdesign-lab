03 · THE CODE

HOUSE
RULES

Every kissa has a code. Ours is short and non-negotiable. Break one and the bartender will know.

01

NO TALKING DURING SOLOS

Whisper during the head, silence during the solo. If you must speak, wait for the applause that never comes. This is a listening bar.

02

PHONES STAY IN POCKETS

No photos of the platter, no Shazam, no scrolling glow in the dark. The music is the screen.

03

ONE REQUEST PER NIGHT

Write it on the slip, hand it to the bar. Make it count. Obscure B-sides earn nods; requests for the obvious earn smiles.

04

THE BAR CHOOSES THE SIDE

You pick the record, we pick the side. Trust the needle. It has been doing this longer than you.

05

STAY FOR THE WHOLE SIDE

Leaving mid-side is leaving mid-sentence. Finish your pour, flip with us, then go.
