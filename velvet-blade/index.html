No. I
Entry the First

The Hot Towel Opening.

We start where great shaves start: heat. Steamed towels open the pores while pre-shave oil softens everything underneath.

By the time the lather goes on, your skin has already surrendered. Heat, steel, patience. That is the whole recipe.

  • Two steamed towel applications
  • Eucalyptus pre-shave oil
  • Badger brush, hot lather
No. II
Entry the Second

Straight Razor. Two Passes.

No cartridges, no shortcuts. A honed straight razor, first with the grain, then against it for the closest finish skin can take.

Between passes: more heat, more lather. Your barber stretches the skin, you just breathe.

  • Honed straight razor, stropped in front of you
  • Two full passes, re-lathered between
  • Alum block to seal and calm
No. III
Entry the Third

Built Blurry.

Then the cut. Skin fade from zero, blended blurry through the crown. Foil shaver on the baseline, razor line-up to frame it.

Guard of your choice on the blend, scissor work on top. Neckline finished blocked, rounded, or tapered, your call.

  • Skin fade, any guard blend
  • Razor line-up, hairline and arches
  • Hot lather neck shave included
No. IV
Entry the Fourth

The Cold Close.

Heat opened the service, cold closes it. Cold towel, aftershave balm, beard oils combed through. Shoulder massage while it all sets.

Final mirror check, full 360. Anything you point at gets fixed before the cape comes off.

  • Cold towel and aftershave balm
  • Beard oil and style product
  • Two minute shoulder massage
